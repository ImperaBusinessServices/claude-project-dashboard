## What's new in v2.9.0

**🟠 Fable on the usage meter.** Anthropic's new Fable model gets its own weekly allowance, so the Week bar now shows an **orange tick** marking how much of Fable's weekly limit you've used — the same numbers as Claude's own usage screen. Hover the meter for the exact percentage. The tray flyout shows it too.

**💸 The Spend section is now collapsible.** By default it shows just the totals row — this month, today, last 7 days, and all time. Click **▸ Details** to expand the daily chart, filters, and breakdown tables. It remembers your choice.
